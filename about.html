About Us

A community built around one belief.

That a child's future should not be limited by poverty, orphanhood, or circumstance.

Who We Are

Donors, volunteers, educators, and caregivers

We are a community of donors, volunteers, educators, and caregivers who believe that a child's future should not be limited by poverty, orphanhood, or circumstance.

Founded in [year — pending confirmation from Nasiri], we began with a simple goal: to make sure no child goes to bed hungry, alone, or without hope.

Today, we support children through homes, schools, health programs, and family support services.

"To make sure no child goes to bed hungry, alone, or without hope."

Our Mission

Every child deserves safety, love, education, and a chance to dream.

At Nasiri Orphanage Center, we work every day to give vulnerable children the support, care, and opportunities they need to grow into healthy, confident, and empowered adults.

Our Values

What guides every decision

Love

We treat every child like our own.

Dignity

We protect each child's worth and potential.

Hope

We believe tomorrow can be better than today.

Transparency

We use every donation with care and share our results openly.

Why It Matters

One life changes a family, a classroom, a community

When you support a child, you don't just change one life. You change a family, a classroom, and a community.

The children we serve are bright, talented, and full of potential. They just need someone to believe in them first.

What We Do

Support that meets real needs

Safe Homes

Shelter, food, clean water, and a loving family environment.

Education

School fees, uniforms, books, and mentorship.

Health & Wellbeing

Medical care, counseling, and daily nutritious meals.

Community Support

Income training, school support, and emergency aid for families.

Who We Support

Nasiri supports orphaned and abandoned children, and the vulnerable families around them, through homes, schools, health programs, and family support services.
