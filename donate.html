Frequently Asked

Good to know

Can I donate by card on this website?

Not yet. This version of the site shares verified donation methods rather than processing payments directly — reach out via the contact form and our team will guide you.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

This depends on Nasiri's registration status, which is pending confirmation and will be published here once verified.

How do I know my gift reached Nasiri?

Message us after you give using the contact form, and our team will confirm receipt with you directly.

Can I sponsor a specific child?

Child sponsorship is coordinated directly with our team to protect each child's privacy and wellbeing — get in touch to learn more.
